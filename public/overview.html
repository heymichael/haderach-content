Arcade Business Platform

What is Arcade Business Platform?

Arcade Business Platform is built for managing business processes in an AI-first world. Vendor management, commodity tracking, sourcing, design and merchandising, marketplace access, market research, trend management, distributed workforce coordination—no matter what domain you need to manage, it can live here in one integrated workspace. Each domain gets a dedicated environment with an AI agent, interactive dashboards, structured data views, and purpose-built modules tailored to that area of the business. No more switching between a forest of disconnected tools. The agent isn't bolted on—it's the primary interface, with traditional views and specialized tools alongside it for when you need them. In addition to providing insight and driving productivity, AI is the connective layer that allows smooth, highly efficient business processes to emerge across every part of your organization.

Core Concepts

The platform is organized around a few building blocks that repeat across every domain.

Domains

Each business area—Vendors, Commodities, and others as they come online—is a self-contained domain with its own AI agent, dashboards, and data views. The left rail lets you switch between domains. Selecting one loads its full workspace.

Agent

Every domain includes a conversational AI assistant. Ask questions in plain English, get instant answers grounded in your data, and make changes through chat. The agent understands the domain's data model and enforces your access controls on every request.

Dashboards

Traditional chart-and-metric views for at-a-glance insights. Dashboards are standalone visual interfaces—they operate independently of the agent and can be filtered by the relevant dimensions for each domain.

Database views

Searchable, sortable tables with inline editing and a detail panel for full record inspection. The database view shares the same underlying data as the agent, so changes made in either place are immediately reflected in the other.

Flexible layout

The toolbar lets you show the agent full-screen, a view full-screen, or both side-by-side. You can switch views while keeping your agent conversation open, making it easy to combine conversational exploration with visual context.

Security & Access Controls

In an AI-driven platform, data can surface in unexpected ways—through a conversational answer, an aggregated chart, or an exported CSV. Fine-grained, server-side access controls are not optional; they are foundational. Every request in Arcade is authenticated, and data visibility is enforced before any results are generated. The AI model only ever sees data the requesting user is authorized to access.

Each domain defines its own access dimensions—departments, individual records, deny lists—and the platform enforces them consistently across the agent, dashboards, database views, CSV exports, and every other surface. Users with administrative roles can bypass filtering when needed. As new domains come online, they inherit the same permissions architecture.

Vendor Management

See and manage everything in one place. No more hopping between Bill.com, Ramp, and a forest of SaaS dashboards.

Key Capabilities

Arcade isn't a replacement for your systems of record—it's the layer that brings them together and turns vendor management into an integrated process between finance and managers. Spending data from payment platforms, vendor details from procurement tools, and contract metadata all flow into a single workspace where everyone is looking at the same data in the same way, each with their own view permissions. Finance sees the full picture; managers see exactly what's relevant to their teams. Ask questions about aggregate spending over time, or drill down into the details of a particular vendor. Focus on the business questions, not on which dashboard to open or who has the login. No more hopping between disconnected tools—everything is integrated, chat-accessible, and governed by a single permissioning system.
